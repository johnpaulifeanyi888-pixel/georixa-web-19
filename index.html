5-Layer Fusion Framework

Multi-Sensor Remote Sensing & Target Discovery

One Earth. Multiple evidence sources. One intelligence layer.

LAYER 1
Satellites

Advanced satellite imaging capturing critical geological and mineralogical data.

LAYER 2
Geophysics

Magnetics, radiometrics, and gravity mapping fault lines, intrusive bodies, density contrasts, and basement structures.

LAYER 3
Geochemistry

Rock, soil, and stream sediment assays integrated with pathfinder element dispersion modeling. Historical datasets.

LAYER 4
Geostructure

Elevation models, ridge lineaments, fault corridors, shear zones, and intrusive morphology.

LAYER 5
AI System

Fuses and synthesizes multi-dimensional data inputs to output evidence-ranked exploration targets.
