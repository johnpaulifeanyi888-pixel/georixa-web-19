One Platform. Multiple Intelligence Layers.

Independent evidence dimensions, synthesized by our AI System.

1. Satellites

Advanced satellite imaging capturing critical geological and mineralogical data.

2. Geophysics

Magnetics, radiometrics, and gravity mapping fault lines and intrusive bodies.

3. Geochemistry

Rock, soil, and stream sediment assays with pathfinder element dispersion modeling.

4. Geostructure

Elevation models, ridge lineaments, fault corridors, and intrusive morphology.

5. AI System

Fuses and synthesizes the 4-layer multi-dimensional data inputs to output high-probability ranked targets.

How It Works

Four steps — from search space to target space.

01

Define the Target

Share your licence or area of interest (AOI) as a KML, shapefile, boundary box coordinates, or map coordinates via WhatsApp or email, along with your target mineral family.

02

Physics-First Data Curation

Our geospatial experts manually curate and validate high-fidelity orbital and geophysical data.

03

Expert-Guided AI Analysis

Our 120+ algorithms, AI Agents, and AI-Stack detect specific minerals and cross-validate independent evidence layers to eliminate false positives and rank high-prospect targets.

04

Deliver the Evidence Ledger

You receive an auditable, ranked report with high-prospect target clusters, coordinates, and georeferenced maps for field follow-up.
