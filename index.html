5-Layer Fusion Framework

Multi-Sensor Remote Sensing & Target Discovery: Independent evidence dimensions, synthesized by our AI System.

1. Satellites

Advanced satellite imaging capturing critical geological and mineralogical data across visible, SWIR, and thermal spectra.

2. Geophysics

Magnetics, radiometrics, and gravity mapping fault lines, intrusive bodies, density contrasts, and basement structures.

3. Geochemistry

Rock, soil, and stream sediment assays integrated with pathfinder element dispersion modeling.

4. Geostructure

Elevation models, ridge lineaments, fault corridors, shear zones, and intrusive morphology.

5. AI System

Fuses and synthesizes multi-dimensional data inputs to output high-probability, evidence-ranked exploration targets.

Evidence architecture

Not every anomaly is a target.

Detect. Challenge. Cross-validate. Prioritize. And when evidence is insufficient, preserve uncertainty.

01 Detect

Identify potentially meaningful spectral, geological, spatial, geophysical or geochemical evidence.

02 Challenge

Evaluate competing explanations and known sources of false positives.

03 Cross-validate

Look for supporting evidence across methods, sensors and independent information.

04 Prioritize

Rank the targets most deserving of the next level of exploration effort.

05 Abstain

When evidence is insufficient or contradictory, GEORIXA® can preserve uncertainty rather than force an answer.

06 Deliver

Produce field-oriented target intelligence with location, evidence state, uncertainty and next checks.
